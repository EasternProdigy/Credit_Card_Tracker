## Netlist errors found (fixed in rev 0.6 of netlist/spec)
1. **AD_EN was tied to PGND.** It is a push-pull driver; datasheet says float. Now no-connect.
2. **ILIM/FOD topology was broken.** Netlist had R1 from `ILIM_N` to PGND and RFOD from FOD to `ILIM_N`, leaving the ILIM ball (net `ILIM`) unconnected. Datasheet Fig. 32 and p.28: ILIM -> R1 -> FOD node -> RFOD -> PGND. Values unchanged (R1 1.55k + RFOD 200R = 1.75k, IMAX = 150 mA).
3. **ROS missing.** TI default: ROS1 20 kOhm RECT -> FOD (ROS2 OUT -> FOD open). TI advises 2 footprints each for ROS and RFOD for calibration. v1 adds ROS1 20k (fit), ROS2 (DNP), RFOD2 (DNP).
