# Q1: onsemi EFC4615R-TR, common-drain dual N-MOSFET (EFCP, 4 bumps)
Source: onsemi EFC4615R datasheet (onsemi.com, "efc4615r-d.pdf"): Package Dimensions 7067-001 and pin list p.1; Electrical Characteristics p.2.

| Functional name (netlist) | Bump | Name |
|---|---|---|
| S1 (to BT_NEG) | 1 | Source1 |
| G1 (DO_GATE) | 2 | Gate1 |
| G2 (CO_GATE) | 3 | Gate2 |
| S2 (to PACK_NEG) | 4 | Source2 |
| D (common drain) | none | internal only; the netlist's `Q1_DRAIN` net is removed |

- Bump map in **bottom view**: 1 top-left, 2 top-right, 4 bottom-left, 3 bottom-right, 0.65 mm pitch, body 1.46 x 1.46 mm.
- Height: 0.22 mm body + 0.15 mm bump = **0.37 mm (typ)**. Inside the 0.625 mm budget and the 0.4 mm target from O6.
- RSS(on) source to source (both FETs in series): 4.5 V 27/31 mOhm typ/max; **3.1 V 33/44 mOhm**; 2.5 V 39/52 mOhm. VSSS 24 V, VGSS ±12 V, IS 6 A.
- With S-82N1BAA VDIOV 0.039 V: discharge overcurrent trips at about 0.89 to 1.18 A at VGS 3.1 V. Our peak load is under 20 mA and charge is 66 mA, so there's plenty of margin.
