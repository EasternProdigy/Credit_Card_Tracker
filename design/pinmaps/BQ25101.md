# U2: TI BQ25101YFPR (DSBGA-6, YFP)
Source: TI bq2510x datasheet (SLUSBV?, 2014), section 6 "Pin Configuration and Functions" p.3 (bq25101/101H column); Device Comparison table p.3; Electrical Characteristics (KISET) p.5; package outline (YFP0006) near end.

| Functional name | Ball | I/O | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| OUT | A1 | O | battery; 1-10 uF |
| IN | A2 | I | 1-10 uF to VSS |
| TS | B1 | I | 10k NTC to VSS; float/high = TTDM; low disables |
| ISET | B2 | I | RISET = KISET/IOUT, KISET 135 typ (129-145) A·Ohm, 20-250 mA |
| CHG | C1 | O | open drain, low = charging (bq25101 only; bq25100 has PRE-TERM here) |
| VSS | C2 | - | ground |

- VO(REG) = 4.20 V (bq25101), VOVP 6.5 V, TS JEITA.
- Package: 0.5 mm MAX seated height; D 1.547-1.608 mm, E 0.85-0.91 mm, 0.4 mm pitch. Passes the 0.625 mm budget.
- R2 2.05k -> 66 mA typ (63-71 mA over KISET spread). Unchanged.
