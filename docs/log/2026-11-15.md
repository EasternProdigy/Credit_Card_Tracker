## Session 00:45: V5_CHG routed, v1 DRC clean
**Who:** Dwayne + KiCad Claude
**Board / area:** v1

### What we did
- Dwayne asked why V5_CHG wouldn't route and to route it, since the board is being ordered tonight.
- Plotted all four copper layers around the gap (x 120 to 186, y 100 to 154).
- Wrote `hardware/v1/gen/route_net.py`, a single-net A* maze router on a 0.05 mm grid. Obstacles come from the real copper shapes (pcbnew `TransformShapeToPolygon`), inflated by 0.18 mm clearance + half the track width. Keep-outs block tracks; vias need hole-to-hole clearance on all layers. It found a route in 5 s with no new vias.
- Added it as 17 segments of 0.3 mm (PWR class) on In2.Cu, from the island A stub end (162.52, 137.27) north along x ≈ 161 to y 127.4, then west to the charger-side In2 track at x = 147.83. Path saved in `hardware/v1/route/v5_chg_hand_path.json`.
- Regenerated the review package (`hardware/v1/review/`: gerbers, drill, CPL, BOM, schematic PDF, DRC, render).

### Why
- Close the last open before ordering.

### Results and measurements
- **Why Freerouting failed:** In1.Cu is a solid GND plane (no signals), so only 3 layers route. The coil keep-out (Ø26.9 mm, all layers) blocks the top and the antenna keep-out blocks the bottom. The only way from JP1 (right) to U2/C12 (left) is a strip about 1 mm wide on In2.Cu between the coil keep-out and the top of U5's decoupling caps, or through the U5 fan-out on In2/B.Cu. Freerouting stalls once its score stops improving (pass 18) and never ripped up enough to use that strip.
- **First attempt failed:** I tidied the router's corners into clean 45° lines by hand. DRC then showed 1 short (VDD_NRF via at (162.05, 136.8)), 1 clearance violation (VBAT_SENSE_MCU via at (160.33, 128.05)) and 1 track crossing. I reverted it and used the router's own corners instead.
- Final DRC with schematic parity: **0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 footprint errors**; the only violations are the 7 known courtyard-overlap warnings (U5 with C20/C22/C23/Y2/L3, L3/C25, L4/C25).
- V5_CHG current is about 66 mA (BQ25101 ICHG = 135 / 2050 Ω), so 0.3 mm on an inner layer is well within capacity.
- BOM: 2 parts are not in JLC stock and are already flagged: U3 S-82N1BAA-I6T1U7 and U4 TPS7A0233PDQNR (Global Sourcing or consign).

### Decisions
- The V5_CHG route is fixed in `card_v1.kicad_pcb`. Rerunning the generator pipeline (`gen_pcb.py` → `route_best.py`) drops it; rerun `route_net.py` afterward if that ever happens.

### Problems and surprises

  - Silkscreen text size.

- **Fixed:** the 11 DNP parts (C1d, C2c, C32, C33, C34, J2, J3, J5, RFOD2, ROS2, R_TSD) were marked DNP but still included in the BOM and position files, so KiCad's JLC export would have listed them for placement. ### Why
- Make the KiCad JLC export produce a BOM and CPL that only contain parts JLC can place.

### Results and measurements
- DRC (schematic parity, all severities): 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 footprint errors. Unchanged after a zone refill.
- 7 courtyard-overlap warnings, measured as pad-to-pad copper gaps:
  - **Cross-net, tightest:** C22 (VDD_NRF) to U5 pin 21 (P2.10, unused) at ≥0.14 mm; C20 (VDD_NRF) to U5 pin 11 (P2.00, unused) at 0.15 mm. A bridge there only ties an unused pin to VDD. Firmware must leave P2.00 and P2.10 as disconnected inputs.
  - **Same-net:** U5 to C22 at 0.10 mm and U5 to C20 at 0.15 mm, both on VDD_NRF, so a bridge is harmless.
  - **The rest:** all ≥0.28 mm.
- ERC: 0 violations. Outline 85.60 × 53.98 mm.
- Stackup: 0.035 / 0.0994 prepreg / 0.0152 / 0.4808 core / 0.0152 / 0.0994 / 0.035 = 0.80 mm, which matches JLC04081H-3313; ENIG.
- Tracks: narrowest 0.112 mm, above JLC's 0.09 mm minimum.
- Vias:
  - 272 × 0.5/0.25.
  - 10 × 0.3/0.15 and 6 × 0.25/0.15 (U1 via-in-pad). These need JLC's 0.15 mm via option.
- RF chain 0.20 mm on F.Cu (0.25 mm on the sub-1 mm shunt stubs).
- Silkscreen: no text below 0.8 mm height or 0.15 mm stroke.
- Assembled parts without an LCSC number: U3 S-82N1BAA-I6T1U7 and U4 TPS7A0233PDQNR (Global Sourcing or consign).
- Only through-hole part in the assembly BOM: J6, the 2.54 mm UART header (C37208).
- Review package regenerated.
